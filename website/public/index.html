JARVIS

Live AI coaching for technical interviews.

Jarvis is an open-source AI interview coach for macOS. It follows both sides of live technical conversations and shows proactive hints in an on-screen overlay. Ask for help out loud or simply keep talking.

Download for macOS

macOS 15 or later · Apple silicon or Intel
Requires AI provider setup

What can Jarvis help with?

Jarvis coaches coding questions, system design, and behavioral interviews. It listens through the microphone and system audio and can check your screen for context.

How does Jarvis handle privacy?

Your configured AI providers may receive audio, text, or screenshots. The overlay is designed to stay out of screen shares and recordings, though some capture methods can show it. Read the privacy guide.
