Design question: redesign the /search service for a hundred times today's traffic.

Agreed so far
- Functional requirements: users search the product catalog by keyword; results are ranked and paginated.
- Non-functional requirements: 20,000 searches per second at peak, p99 under 300 ms, 99.9% availability, results may be up to one minute stale.
- Core entities: Product, SearchIndex, Query.
- API: GET /search?q=...&page=... returns ranked product IDs with title snippets.

Next step: sketch the high-level architecture. Which components would you put in, and what is the read path for one request?
